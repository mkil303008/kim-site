Strategy, service and giving back

Kimberly Evans is Executive Vice President and Head of Enterprise Strategic Relationships at Northern Trust. She brings the firm's full capabilities together to deliver integrated solutions for large, complex client, prospect and vendor relationships around the world.

Her 25+ years in financial services span business strategy, P&L leadership, client servicing, technology, operations and change management. Before Northern Trust she held senior roles at American National Bank, First Chicago NBD and ABN AMRO/LaSalle Bank, and was Chief Operating Officer of Diamond Bank, which later became part of Wintrust. She also ran her own consulting business advising community bank leaders and entrepreneurs.

At Northern Trust she has led its Private Capital Fund Services business in North America, its public funds and sovereign wealth business, and its work on sustainability, inclusion and social impact. She is known as a business transformation expert, and sought after as a speaker for her authentic leadership and wide experience. She holds a Bachelor of Science in Psychology from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.
